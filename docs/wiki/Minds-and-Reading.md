# Minds & Reading

Duragon is designed to sit quietly beside your writing instead of taking over the page.

## Reading Modes

Switch any note's mode from the header row above the title (or set the default for new notes in **Customize → General**):

- **Thinks with you:** After you pause typing, Duragon reads the newly changed paragraph. Most of the time it stays silent; when it spots a factual error, gap, contradiction, or unexamined assumption, it places a quiet question in the margin anchored to the exact phrase.
- **When you ask:** Completely silent until you explicitly ask — by starting a line with `Duragon,` (or your active Mind's name), selecting text, or opening the side chat (`Ctrl+J`).

## Inline Answers & Sketches

- Start any paragraph with `Duragon,` (or the name of the active Mind) and press `Enter` to get an answer directly underneath your line.
- Ask it to *draw*, *sketch*, *diagram*, or *visualize* something to generate a step-by-step hand-drawn SVG right inside the note. Use the step controls (`< 1/3 >`) to step through or replay the diagram, or click **See it larger** to open it in the lightbox.
- Click any inline answer to continue the thread right there, or open its `⋯` menu to move it to the margin.

## Margin Placement & Just My Words

In **Customize → General**, choose where margin questions appear:

- **Both margins** — your text centered, questions on either side.
- **Right margin** — all questions in one column on the right.
- **Marks only** — a small ink dot inline after the passage; click it to open.

Toggle **Just my words** (`Ctrl+Shift+.` or `za` in Vim mode) at any time to hide all margin marks and threads temporarily while you focus on writing.

## Custom Minds

Open **Customize → Minds** to create custom reviewers (for example, *Staff Engineer*, *Editor*, *Skeptic*, or *Tutor*). Each Mind has:

- **Name & description**
- **How it thinks** — its perspective and what it looks for
- **Tone** — Plain, Warm, Blunt, or Riddle
- **Frequency** — Rarely, Sometimes, or Often

Pick a Mind per note from the first dropdown in the note header row.

## Per-Note Briefs & About You

- **Tell Duragon** (note header row): Attach a short per-note brief (audience, goal, constraints) that stays in context for every AI call on that note.
- **About you** (**Customize → About you**): Background about yourself that Duragon keeps in mind across all notes.
