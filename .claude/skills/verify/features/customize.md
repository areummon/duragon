# Customize

The Customize dialog holds seven focused pages: `General`, `Appearance`, `Providers`, `Minds`, `About you`, `Voice` and `Help`. Reading and deleted-notes settings live on `General`; theme, text size, page width, lettering and colour live on `Appearance`; Models, Providers and Web search live on `Providers`; voice settings and local voice models live together on `Voice`. Settings save as they change. There is no Save button, except for an API key and for a mind. From Minds, the writer opens the mind editor to make or change a mind.

## Sub-features

- `customize-open` opens the dialog from the keyboard and the sidebar's Settings menu.
- `customize-switch` toggles a setting and stores it. On `General` the switches are `Web search in new notes` (`webInNewNotes`), `Search my other notes in chat` (`searchMyNotes`) and `Explain symbols in formulas` (`explainSymbols`); on `Voice` is `New notes speak aloud` (`voiceInNewNotes`), all off by default. Under `Notes` on `General`, `Deleted notes` picks one of three radios (`deletedNotes`, `trash.md`). `About you` has `Duragon learns about you` (`learnAboutYou`, on by default).
- `customize-choices` picks between the radios of `General` and `Appearance`, and stores the pick at once. The defaults are marked with a star. On `General`: `newNoteMode`: `Thinks with you`* (`thinks`) or `When you ask` (`asks`); `readingPace`: `Regular`* or `Slow`; `questionPlacement`: `Both margins`*, `Right margin` or `Marks only` (`marks`). On `Appearance`: `appearance`: `System`*, `Light` or `Dark`; `textSize`: `Small`, `Default`* or `Large`; `lettering`: `Duragon’s own`* or `Like your notes` (`notes`); `inkColour`: `Ink`*, `Blue`, `Green`, `Red`, `Purple`, `Brown` or `Orange`. `Reset` by `Page width` is disabled until the width was dragged. On `Voice`, the voice and speech-to-text pickers are `customize-voice`, and `speakingSpeed` is a slider in steps of 0.05, at `1.00×`*.
- `customize-about` keeps the paragraph `About you` (`aboutYou`, at most 700 characters).
- `customize-models` picks the model, its provider and the thinking level for `Reading`, `Chat` and `Drawing` (`readModel`, `readProvider`, `readThinking` and so on), on the `Providers` page.
- `customize-providers` lists the providers that are set up or in use, adds, changes and removes them, and configures web search engines and keys on the `Providers` page.
- `customize-voice` picks the voice (`voiceName`) and the speech-to-text model (`listenModel`) from two rows on `Voice`, `Voice` and `Speech to text`, each showing its pick over its engine. Each opens a dialog of the same name, like the Models rows: the combobox `Find a voice` or `Find a model`, a listbox grouped by engine and source (`Kokoro · Duragon`, `Pico · Speech Dispatcher`, `Parakeet · Duragon`, `whisper.cpp · on this computer`), a check `In use`, and `Get more voices` or `Get more models`, which scrolls to the `Models` section below on the same page. Arrows, Home, End and Enter pick from the keyboard; Escape and a click outside close it. The highlighted voice has a button `Play a sample of …`, which plays without picking and turns into `Stop the sample of …`. A pick that is gone falls back to the first in the list. With nothing installed the dialog says so.
- `customize-pages` moves between `General`, `Appearance`, `Providers`, `Minds`, `About you`, `Voice` and `Help`, resetting scroll to the top on each switch. Help lists the shortcuts, including ```, `Esc`, `Tab` and `Shift Tab`.
- `customize-minds` opens the mind editor from `New Mind` on the Minds page or from the mind picker in the note header.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Press `Control+,` (`Cmd+,` on macOS). It toggles, and it reopens on the last page shown. While another dialog, such as the mind editor or the command palette, is open, it does nothing.
- Choose `Open notes`, then `Settings` at the bottom of the sidebar, then the menuitem `Customize Ctrl ,` (`find role menuitem click --name "Customize"` matches it), `Providers` or `Minds`. The menu always opens on that page.
- The mind picker in the note header (button `Duragon`) has the menuitems `New Mind` and `Manage`.
- `Close`, Escape or a click on the scrim closes it. Escape closes the newest layer first, so with the mind editor open, the first Escape closes the editor and the second closes Customize.

## Driving it with agent-browser

Preconditions:

- Baseline state from `README.md`.

- **Open.** Run `agent-browser press Control+,`, and `agent-browser wait '[aria-label="Settings sections"]'`. The navigation `Settings sections` (`General`, `Appearance`, `Providers`, `Minds`, `About you`, `Voice`, `Help`) and the button `Close` appear, and `[aria-modal] .scroll` is at its top, the first time too.
- **Toggle.** On `General`, run `agent-browser find role switch click --name "Explain symbols in formulas"`. The switch reports `checked=true` in `agent-browser snapshot -i`.
- **Stored.** Read `(await b.getSettings()).explainSymbols` with the store eval from `../SKILL.md`. It goes from `false` to `true`.
- **Appearance.** On `Appearance`, choose `Dark`, then `Light`. The whole window fades in 250 ms, with `ui-fade` on `::view-transition-new(root)`. `document.getAnimations()` also lists the browser’s own animation of the group. Choosing what already shows starts no transition.
- **Choices.** On `General`, pick `When you ask`, `Slow`, `Marks only`; on `Appearance`, pick `Large`, `Like your notes` and `Blue`; on `Voice`, search `Voice` for `eng` and pick `Serena` with ArrowDown and Enter. `__duragon.settings.value` has `asks`, `slow`, `marks`, `large`, `notes`, `blue` and `speechd/pico/serena`, and `document.documentElement.dataset` has `textSize`, `lettering` and `ink`. A note made with `Control+n` then has the mode `asks`.
- **Speed and width.** On `Voice`, focus the slider with `agent-browser eval 'document.querySelector("[aria-modal] input[type=range]").focus()'` and press ArrowRight. `speakingSpeed` is 1.05. On `Appearance`, with the page width dragged to 770, choose `Reset`. `pageWidth` is `null`.
- **About you.** On `About you`, fill the textbox and wait 600 ms. `aboutYou` holds it, and the page counts `16 of 700`. On a fresh load, without opening Customize, let Duragon learn something with `b.settings.aboutYou = "X"; b.emit("about:updated", "X")`, then open the sidebar, which saves the settings. `aboutYou` is still `X`. On a fresh load, focus the textbox, learn the same, and press Tab. It is still `X`. Type "mine" there while an earlier save is held up (make the first `b.saveSettings` wait 2.5 s and toggle a switch), then learn `X`. `aboutYou` ends as `mine`, also with another setting changed meanwhile.
- **Pages.** Choose each button in `Settings sections`. `Minds` shows `New Mind`. `About you` shows the switch `Duragon learns about you`. `Voice` shows `New notes speak aloud`, `Voice`, `Speech to text`, `Speaking speed`, and under `Models`: `Install Parakeet`, `Install Silero VAD`, `Install Kokoro` and, under `On this computer`, `Speech Dispatcher` and `whisper.cpp`.
- **Mind editor.** On `Minds`, choose `New Mind` and wait for the dialog. A dialog named `New Mind` opens over Customize with the textboxes `Name`, `Description …` and `How it thinks …`, and `Cancel`. The last two names carry their hint, so leave out `--exact`. It also has chips under `Start from` (`Staff engineer` to `Blank`) and radios under `Voice` and `How often it speaks up`. Press Escape. `__duragon.app.mindEditor` is `null` and `__duragon.app.customizeOpen` is still `true`. Press Escape again, and Customize closes.
- **Save a mind.** On `Minds`, choose `New Mind`, fill `Name` and `How it thinks`, and press `Control+Enter`. `listRiddlers` gains it, and only the editor closes. Started from the note's mind picker, it also becomes the note's mind (`note-header.md`).
- **Pick a model.** On `Providers`, focus the `Drawing` row with `agent-browser eval '[...document.querySelectorAll(".job .row")].find((b) => b.textContent.includes("Drawing")).focus()'` and press Enter. The dialog `Drawing model` opens, and its combobox `Find a model, or type its name` has focus. Arrows, Home and End move through the listbox `Models`. Type `haiku` and press Enter. The dialog closes, the row has focus, and `drawProvider` and `drawModel` change together in `__duragon.settings.value`.
- **Any name.** Type a name no provider lists, such as `glm-5.2`. The group `Use “glm-5.2” with` offers each ready provider, the current one first. A command added without models lists `default`. An API provider added without models shows the disabled option `Type the name of its model`, and Enter on it does nothing.
- **Thinking.** In the dialog press Tab. The radiogroup `Thinking` (`Auto`, `Low`, `Medium`, `High`, `Highest`) is one tab stop, the arrows pick, and the dialog stays open. One more Tab reaches `Set up another provider`, which opens the Add menu.
- **Escape.** With the dialog open, Escape closes only that. `__duragon.app.customizeOpen` stays `true`.
- **Save a key.** Choose `Add` (with `--exact`), then the menuitem `OpenRouter`. Its row appears open, with focus in `API key`. Type a key and press Enter. The row shows the key masked, and `(await b.listCredentialStatuses())` has it under `hasStoredKey`.
- **Key that won't save.** Stub the page's backend with `b.setProviderKey = async () => { throw "no space left on device"; }`, and save again. Undo the stub afterwards with `delete b.setProviderKey`. An alert says `The key wasn’t saved: no space left on device. Your old key is still in place.`, and the field keeps the draft.
- **Own provider.** Choose `Add`, then `Something else…`. Pick the radio `A command`, fill `Name` and `Command`, and press Enter. (An `OpenAI` provider with no key and no `Models` can’t be given a job, because the model list doesn’t have it.) Give the `Drawing` job its model `default`, then choose `Remove provider` in its row. The row goes, the row above has focus, and the job is on `claude-cli` with a Claude model.
- **Setting that won't save.** Stub `b.saveSettings = async () => { throw "disk full"; }` and toggle a switch. `delete b.saveSettings` undoes it. It goes back, and a toast says `That change couldn’t be saved.` A provider that can't be removed says so in its row instead, with no toast.
- **Command or address that isn't allowed.** The app asks before a new command, new options or a new address is saved, in a native window, so only the real window proves the question. Make the page's `b.saveSettings` throw `"The change was not allowed"` and change `Claude CLI`'s `Command`. It goes back, and a toast says `That wasn’t allowed, so it wasn’t saved.` A command added under `Something else…` goes away, and `Add` has focus.
- **Keys that can't be read.** Choose `Add` and `OpenRouter` first, because only API providers that are listed show it. Make `b.listCredentialStatuses` return `error` on each API provider, and change a provider's address. The reason stands under `Providers`, and those rows say `Key can’t be read`.
- **Local servers.** Open Customize and switch to `Providers`. The mock's Ollama answers, so its row reads `Ollama 2 models` and the picker offers `qwen3:8b`. Choose `Add`, then `llama.cpp` under `On this computer`. Its row reads `Can’t be reached`, and an alert under `Address` says `Nothing answers at http://localhost:8080/v1.` Change Ollama's `Address`, and its row reads the same until it is changed back. `Check` asks again.
- **Proof.** Take screenshots before and after the toggle, and an `.aria.txt` of the dialog.

## Gotchas

- `Control+,` reopens the last page shown; choose the target page in `Settings sections` first if needed.
- The `Voice` and `Web` switches in the note header have the same role and live outside the dialog. Match the full switch name.
- `About you` is also the name of a textbox on that page. Use `role button` to reach the page.
- While the mind editor sits over Customize, two `Close` buttons exist.
- The dialog has one scroller, `.jobs`' closest `.scroll`. A bare `.scroll` is the side chat's.
- The model list holds only ready providers and the current one, so save a key first to see an API provider in it. Every command line provider counts as ready, because nothing checks that its command exists. A local server counts as ready while it answers.
- Settings in the mock reset on reload.
